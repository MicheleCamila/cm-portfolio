IntelliSecure :AI-Powered SMS Fraud Detection System 🏆 Hackathon Winner

SMS fraud and mobile money scams are exploding across Kenya and East Africa, targeting unsuspecting mobile users daily. Traditional security measures can't keep pace with evolving fraud tactics.IntelliSecure leverages AI to analyze SMS messages in real-time, detecting fraudulent patterns before users fall victim.The system learns continuously from user feedback, adapting to new threats instantly.

Web Application

E-Laundry

A web-based platform that streamlines the laundry order and management process at Egerton University. The platform enables students to submit and track laundry orders online while giving management staff a dashboard to process orders, assign workloads, and generate reports. The goal is to improve service efficiency, eliminate manual record-keeping, reduce revenue leakage, and create a transparent order history for every student.

mobile Application

Smart Market - your AI business companion

Currently the Government of Kenya has an ongoing project known as NYOTA, a project that is meant to absorb youths between the age 18-29 with education level of Form four and below (inclusive of dropouts). NYOTA is offering business support to enable MSMESs to grow and create jobs. Smart Market is a mobile application that connects potential beneficiaries with local suppliers, customers, and business resources. The app provides a platform for users to list products/services, access market information, network with other enterpreneurs. Market intelligence means understanding your business environment what your customers want, what competitors are doing, how prices are shifting and the ongoing trends Smart Market uses AI to automatically collect, analyze and interpret this data so that MSMES can make smarter, faster, impactful and more profitable decision.
